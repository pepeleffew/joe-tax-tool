Baseball
Supplemental 1st Round Pick · Boys Hitting & Pitching
David Mead

Born and raised in Chattanooga, David Mead became one of the most decorated high school pitchers in Tennessee history at Soddy-Daisy High School — a 6'5" right-hander with a live arm that put him on every major league radar. In 1999, the Texas Rangers selected him 47th overall in the supplemental first round of the MLB Draft. He spent six seasons in professional baseball across the Texas Rangers and San Diego Padres organizations — pitching in the Gulf Coast League, Appalachian League (Pulaski Rangers), South Atlantic League (Savannah Sand Gnats), California League (Stockton Ports), and Midwest League (Fort Wayne Wizards) — logging 115 career appearances and 381 professional innings. Now he's back home in Hixson, bringing big-league mechanics, a pitcher's deep understanding of hitters, and first-hand knowledge of what it takes to get drafted — to every session he coaches.

Softball
UTC Hall of Fame · Girls Hitting & Pitching
Michelle LaCourse

One of the most decorated players in UT Chattanooga softball history, Michelle starred at UTC from 2008–2012 as a pitcher and first baseman. She was named SoCon Freshman of the Year in 2008, SoCon Player of the Year in 2011 and 2012, and became the first softball player ever to earn SoCon Female Athlete of the Year honors. She holds UTC career records in RBIs (217) and total bases (453), and set single-season records in slugging (.846), on-base percentage (.543), and RBIs (75). After her playing career she joined the UTC coaching staff, developing multiple award-winning pitchers and guiding the Mocs to SoCon championships. Inducted into the Southern Conference Hall of Fame (2022) and the UTC Hall of Fame (2023), she continues to shape the game through coaching, broadcasting for the SEC, Southern Conference, and NFHS Network, and leadership with the Scenic City Collegiate League. A Hixson local, she brings all of that to your athlete.
