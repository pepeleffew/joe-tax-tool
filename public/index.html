Powered By Pro-Level Tech

Big-league data. Right here in Hixson.

Most kids never see the numbers behind their swing. Ours do — every session. Strike Zone Chattanooga is proud to be one of the only facilities in the area training on HitTrax, alongside Rapsodo/TrackMan tracking and high-speed video. Real metrics, instant feedback, measurable progress.

Featuring Live in the Cage

HitTrax SimulatorFlagship

Camera-based hit tracking that measures exit velocity, launch angle, distance and point of impact in real time — then launches the ball through virtual MLB stadiums for games, home-run derbies and a national leaderboard.

Rapsodo / TrackMan

Pitch- and swing-tracking radar for the numbers that win at the next level: pitch velocity, spin rate, movement, and swing data for both hitters and pitchers.

High-Speed Video Analysis

Frame-by-frame slow-motion breakdown of every swing and delivery, so coaches can show — not just tell — exactly what to fix and reinforce what's working.

Good To Know

Questions, answered.

Yes. Strike Zone is built for both. Boys train hitting and pitching with our former-pro baseball coach, and girls train hitting and pitching with our elite collegiate-level softball coach.
