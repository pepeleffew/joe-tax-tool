Since 1973
’73Half a century of craft

“Big enough to handle the volume, close enough to still pick up the phone — and still doing the decoration by hand, in Chattanooga.”

We opened in 1973 and never outgrew the craft. Every logo is digitized once and archived forever; every press run is matched to the garment, the art, and the deadline. That's how a youth-league order and a college program come out looking equally sharp.

01 · Method

Screen Print

Bold, opaque, durable color at volume. Unbeatable cost per piece on tees and jerseys.

Best for · high quantity
02 · Method

Embroidery

Stitched dimension and a premium hand. Built to last for years on polos, caps, and outerwear.

Best for · sideline & staff
03 · Method

Sublimation

Dye fused into the fabric for full-coverage designs that never crack or peel.

Best for · game uniforms
04 · Method

Tackle Twill & DTF

Sewn-on twill for a varsity look; direct-to-film for full-color, no-minimum add-ons.
