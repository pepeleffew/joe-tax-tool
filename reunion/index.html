Our Senior Year

  • Bill Clinton inaugurated as the 42nd President (Jan 20)
  • Grunge ruled — flannel, Doc Martens & mixtapes
  • The Mosaic web browser launched — the internet went mainstream
  • Intel shipped the first Pentium chip
🎵

On the Radio

  • “I Will Always Love You” — Whitney Houston
  • “Whoomp! (There It Is)” — Tag Team
  • “That’s the Way Love Goes” — Janet Jackson
  • “Dreamlover” — Mariah Carey
  • “A Whole New World” — from Aladdin
🎬

At the Movies

  • Jurassic Park — #1 at the box office
  • Mrs. Doubtfire
  • The Fugitive
  • Sleepless in Seattle
  • Groundhog Day & Schindler’s List
📺

On TV

  • Cheers aired its finale (42M viewers)
  • The X-Files premiered
  • Mighty Morphin Power Rangers debuted
  • Seinfeld, Home Improvement & 90210

In Sports

  • Cowboys won Super Bowl XXVII
  • Michael Jordan & the Bulls “three-peated”
  • Joe Carter’s walk-off HR won the Blue Jays the World Series
